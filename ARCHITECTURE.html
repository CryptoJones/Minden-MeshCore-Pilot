MINDEN MESH PILOT · off-grid community messaging · Minden, NE

DONATED — City of Minden PERSONAL — not city infrastructure MeshCore Meshtastic · RX only

One donated node. Everything else is yours.

The city receives a single sealed solar repeater on the siren pole and is asked to host nothing else — no computer, no account, no maintenance. The historian, the logs, and the monitoring all live on personal hardware in a personal window, on personal power.

That boundary is the design. It keeps the civic footprint to the smallest thing a city can say yes to, and it settles the data question before anyone raises it: nothing the city owns is collecting anything.

The pole gets hardened hardware for four reasons: lithium cannot be charged below 0 °C without permanent damage and Minden sees −10 °C winters; the battery is the very thing under test, so an undersized pack makes the pilot fail for reasons unrelated to mesh; one bucket-truck visit costs more than the hardware saved; and off-the-shelf rated gear clears a city facilities review in a way a hand-built node does not.

What gets collected, and how

A MeshCore repeater keeps no history — it answers with an instantaneous snapshot and forgets. The Pi is therefore the single source of truth for anything time-series, and it collects two different ways:

MODECOSTS AIRTIME?WHAT IT YIELDS
PassiveNoAdvertisements, SNR/RSSI, hop paths, node census. Reachability derives from observed traffic — free, and it degrades honestly.
PolledYesBattery, uptime counters — the things advertisements don't carry. Authenticated, sparing.

Passive first, poll sparingly. A repeater that stops answering polls might merely be busy; a repeater that stops advertising is genuinely gone. The free signal is also the more truthful one.

Bulk history never moves over the mesh — a LoRa message is a few hundred bytes and rate-limited. Over the air you get a one-message summary; the complete dataset is pulled off the Pi directly over the home LAN, which is trivial now that the Pi lives on it.

Design decisions

IDDECISIONRATIONALE
DD-001Community messagingNot "emergency comms". Emergency resilience is the wedge for pitching a city; carrier-free everyday communication is the mission.
DD-002Bridge is historianThe repeater has no memory, so the Pi polls and persists. Outages stored as reachable=0 so gaps are real data.
DD-003Summaries over airOne question, one short answer. No raw rows, no pagination. Detail comes off the Pi out-of-band.
DD-004MeshCore onlyGreenfield with no Meshtastic population. Bridge code kept, not deleted — enabling it should be config, not a rewrite.
DD-005Two sitesSolar repeater on the pole, mains historian in a window. Evaluation data without a second install on the pole.
DD-006Two hardware tiersHardened pole unit; cheap coverage nodes everywhere accessible. Density is the product, cost per node is the ceiling.
DD-007Donation is one nodeThe Pi is personal instrumentation. The Meshtastic receiver is a survey instrument that measures DD-004 instead of assuming it.
